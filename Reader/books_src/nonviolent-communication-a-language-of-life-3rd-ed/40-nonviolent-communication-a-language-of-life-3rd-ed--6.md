# _Requesting That Which Would Enrich Life_

We have now covered the first three components of NVC, which address what we are _observing, feeling,_ and _needing._ We have learned to do this without criticizing, analyzing, blaming, or diagnosing others, and in a way likely to inspire compassion. The fourth and final component of this process addresses _what we would like to request of others_ in order to enrich life for us. When our needs are not being fulfilled, we follow the expression of what we are observing, feeling, and needing with a specific request: we ask for actions that might fulfill our needs. How do we express our requests so that others are more willing to respond compassionately to our needs?
