**The Mask**

_Always a mask_

_Held in the slim hand whitely_

_Always she had a mask before her_

_face—_

_Truly the wrist_

_Holding it lightly_

_Fitted the task:_

_Sometimes however_

_Was there a shiver,_

_Fingertip quiver,_

_Ever so slightly—_

_Holding the mask?_

_For years and years and years I_

_wondered_

_But dared not ask_

_And then—_

_I blundered,_

_Looked behind the mask,_

_To find_

_Nothing—_

_She had no face._

_She had become_

_Merely a hand_

_Holding a mask_

_With grace._

—Author unknown
