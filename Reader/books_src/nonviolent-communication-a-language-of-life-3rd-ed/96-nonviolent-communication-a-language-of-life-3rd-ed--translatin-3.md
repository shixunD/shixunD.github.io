# **Translating “No”**

When we express a request, it’s very important to be respectful of the other person’s reaction, whether or not they agree to our request. Many mediations I have witnessed consist of waiting for people to wear down to the point where they’ll accept any compromise. This is very different from a resolution in which everyone’s needs are met and nobody experiences loss.

In [Chapter 8](part0014_split_000.html#ch-8), we discovered the importance of not hearing “no” as rejection. Listening carefully to the message behind the “no” helps us understand the other person’s needs: _When they say “no,” they’re saying they have a need that keeps them from saying “yes” to what we are asking._ If we can hear the need behind a “no,” we can continue the conflict resolution process—maintaining our focus on finding a way to meet everybody’s needs—even if the other party says “no” to the particular strategy we presented them.
